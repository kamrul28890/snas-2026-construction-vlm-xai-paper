\section{Metrics}

The benchmark separates \emph{what} a model answers from \emph{where} it looks and \emph{whether} that evidence affects the answer.

\paragraph{Answer metrics.}
For a reference label $y_i$ and normalized prediction $\hat{y}_i$, we report accuracy, macro-F1 over \texttt{compliant}, \texttt{violation}, and \texttt{uncertain}, invalid-output rate, and nonambiguous-example accuracy. Bootstrap-label scores measure pipeline consistency; final-audit scores measure behavior against the completed 120-row audit layer.

\paragraph{Evidence metrics.}
For predicted boxes $\hat{B}$ and reference boxes $B$, we report evidence presence, best IoU, normalized centroid drift, and evidence disappearance. IoU alone is insufficient because small PPE regions can produce large IoU swings from tiny localization errors; centroid drift and disappearance expose evidence relocation even when overlap is hard to interpret.

\paragraph{Intervention metrics.}
Each row with a target box receives one rule-evidence occlusion and five same-size matched-random occlusions. The key paired statistic is $\Delta_{\mathrm{target-random}}$: the targeted answer-flip or evidence-drift effect minus the per-example matched-random mean. A faithful evidence-using model should change more under targeted masks than under matched random masks, but should not collapse to arbitrary evidence elsewhere.
