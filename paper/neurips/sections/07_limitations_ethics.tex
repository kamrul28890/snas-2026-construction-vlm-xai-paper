\section{Limitations and Ethics}

The study is a compact audit artifact, not a complete field-deployment benchmark. First, the 120-row final-label layer is complete but has explicit AI-pass provenance: two independent AI-generated passes plus returned adjudication for disagreements. It should be used as an adjudicated audit layer, not described as unqualified human ground truth. Second, the only current image-grounded model is a Florence-2 grounding adapter with deterministic decision logic; stronger instruction-following VLMs and closed-model adapters must be added before broad model-ranking claims. Third, the scale-up split remains imbalanced because struck-by and fall-hazard source examples are scarce.

Construction-site imagery raises privacy, surveillance, and labor-governance concerns. This benchmark must not be used for autonomous inspection, worker discipline, or surveillance. Appropriate use is research evaluation of VLM faithfulness under documented scope limits. Future releases should preserve source licensing, avoid redistributing images when rights are unclear, document non-commercial restrictions, include prohibited-use language, and require human safety professionals for any real inspection workflow.
