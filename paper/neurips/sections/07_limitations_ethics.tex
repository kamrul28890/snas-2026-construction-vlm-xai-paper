\section{Limitations and Ethics}

The current scaffold has three major limitations. First, the completed annotation layer is model-assisted and metadata-derived, not independent human ground truth. Second, the current model outputs are deterministic baselines, not real multi-model VLM evaluations. Third, the pilot sample is only 163 image-rule pairs and should not be treated as a full benchmark.

Construction-site imagery also raises privacy, surveillance, and labor-governance concerns. This benchmark must not be used as an autonomous inspection, worker discipline, or surveillance system. Future releases should preserve source licensing, avoid redistributing images when rights are unclear, document non-commercial restrictions, and include prohibited-use language.
